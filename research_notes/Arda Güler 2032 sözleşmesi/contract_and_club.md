# Arda Güler's Real Madrid contract extension to 2032: verified facts and the club side

Research date: 2026-11-20. Note: realmadrid.com pages returned an error page when fetched, so the club communiqué text below comes from outlets quoting it (El Español, Milliyet), not from a direct read of realmadrid.com. The Mourinho press-conference transcript is on realmadrid.com (URL found via search), but the quotes here come from Libertad Digital's report.

## 1. When was it announced, is it official, and what was the previous end date?

### Takeaway
The extension is **official**. Real Madrid announced it in a club communiqué on **Wednesday 18 November 2026**, and Güler is now tied to the club until **30 June 2032**. The previous contract ran to 2029, so this adds three seasons. The agreement itself had been reported as closed on 10 September 2026 by AS and Fabrizio Romano, 69 days before the announcement.

### Cited Findings
- Official club communiqué, quoted verbatim: "El Real Madrid C. F. y Arda Güler han acordado la ampliación del contrato de nuestro jugador, que queda vinculado al club hasta el 30 de junio de 2032." The article is by Jorge Pacheco, published 18 Nov 2026 at 11:43 — [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/deportes/futbol/20261118/real-madrid-blinda-arda-guler-anuncia-renovacion/1003744462627_0.html)
- Turkish rendering of the same club statement: "Real Madrid ve Arda Güler, oyuncumuzun sözleşmesini uzatma konusunda anlaşmaya vardı". Article dated 18.11.2026 at 13:44 — [Milliyet](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/skorer/real-madridden-arda-guler-icin-sozlesme-aciklamasi-7703045)
- Turkish outlets also report it as official ("resmi açıklama yapıldı"): [NTV Spor](https://www.ntv.com.tr/sporskor/arda-guler-imzayi-atti-resmi-aciklama-yapildi-2032ye-kadar-real-madrid-1751457); [Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/spor/son-dakika-real-madrid-arda-guler-in-sozlesmesini-2032-yilina-kadar-uzattigini-duyurdu-2557211); [Mynet](https://www.mynet.com/real-madrid-resmen-duyurdu-arda-guler-in-sozlesmesi-2032-ye-kadar-uzatildi-526919-myspor)
- English-language coverage: ESPN headline "Arda Güler signs new six-year Real Madrid contract". The page body could not be fetched — [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50519617/arda-guler-signs-new-real-madrid-contract)
- The previous contract expired in 2029, so the extension adds three seasons — [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/sporarena/arda-guler-real-madrid-ile-sozlesme-uzatti-ispanyada-mansetler-patladi-bos-kagida-imza-atti-43383215); [ensonhaber](https://www.ensonhaber.com/real-madrid-arda-gulerin-sozlesmesini-uzattigini-acikladi-h1583548); [Fichajes.net, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.fichajes.net/noticias/confianza-total-arda-guler-20260910.html)
- 10 Sep 2026: AS reported the renewal closed and ready to be announced, to June 2032. Fabrizio Romano posted the same day: "The agreement is done and set to be announced soon — understand it will be valid until June 2032, six years." — [Fichajes.net](https://www.fichajes.net/noticias/confianza-total-arda-guler-20260910.html)
- El Gol Digital says it reported the agreement on 10 September, 69 days before the announcement. It also says reported end dates had moved between 2031, 2032 and (a week before the announcement) 2035 — [El Gol Digital](https://www.elgoldigital.com/futbol/real-madrid-cf/arda-guler-renueva-real-madrid-2032)
- La Vanguardia noted Güler will be 27 when the deal ends (as cited by Hürriyet) — [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/sporarena/arda-guler-real-madrid-ile-sozlesme-uzatti-ispanyada-mansetler-patladi-bos-kagida-imza-atti-43383215)

### Inferences
- "Six years" (Romano, ESPN headline) counts from his 2026 signing to 2032. "Three years" counts the extension beyond the old 2029 end date. Both are accurate; they measure different things. Fichajes.net itself mixes the two framings.
- The exact signing date is not public. The announcement date (18 Nov) is the date to use. The roughly two-month gap after the September "agreement done" reports suggests the details or timing were still being settled.

### Gaps
- I could not read the original realmadrid.com communiqué; the site returned an error page. Its exact URL is still unknown.
- No public date for the physical signing.

## 2. Salary, raise, release clause and bonuses: who reported what

### Takeaway
Real Madrid did **not** disclose salary or release clause. The figures in circulation are press reports or estimates, and they conflict:
- **Salary:** about €8M a year (up from about €6.25M), versus Capology's estimate of €10.42M gross.
- **Release clause:** €800M versus €1,000M.

Treat all of these as unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Salary (Milliyet):** €6.25M a year excluding bonuses under the old deal, rising to €8M a year. Milliyet attributes this only to "İspanyol basını" (the Spanish press) and names no outlet. It also works out about €153,846 a week — [Milliyet](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/skorer/real-madridden-arda-guler-icin-sozlesme-aciklamasi-7703045)
- **Salary and clause (Mynet):** gives the €8M as a net figure and says an €800M release clause "has been reported in the press". Mynet and Milliyet frame the salary differently (net vs. excluding bonuses) — [Mynet](https://www.mynet.com/real-madrid-resmen-duyurdu-arda-guler-in-sozlesmesi-2032-ye-kadar-uzatildi-526919-myspor)
- **El Gol Digital:** the club disclosed neither salary nor clause. It links the €8M salary and €800M clause to the earlier rumoured 2035 proposal and says neither is officially confirmed — [El Gol Digital](https://www.elgoldigital.com/futbol/real-madrid-cf/arda-guler-renueva-real-madrid-2032)
- **Capology estimate:** €5.21M gross fixed salary in 2025-26, rising to €10.42M gross in 2026-27. This is an estimate — [Unánimo Deportes](https://unanimodeportes.com/deportes/futbol/laliga/nuevo-salario-arda-guler-real-madrid/); [La Prensa (HN)](https://www.laprensa.hn/deportes/arda-guler-real-madrid-renovacion-salario-contrato-mercado-fichaje-europa-EP32680602)
- **€1,000M clause:** one outlet says Güler and Tchouaméni are protected with €1,000M release clauses. This conflicts with the €800M figure — [OKDiario](https://okdiario.com/diariomadridista/real-madrid/real-madrid-recoge-frutos-apuesta-guler-tchouameni-678212) (as summarised in search results; not fetched directly)
- **Clause in September:** Fichajes.net reported no information on whether the clause would change — [Fichajes.net](https://www.fichajes.net/noticias/confianza-total-arda-guler-20260910.html)
- **AS headline:** "Firmó en blanco" (Hürriyet's Turkish rendering is "BEYAZ KAĞIDA İMZALADI"; Hürriyet quotes it as "Güler, boş bir kağıda sözleşme imzaladı"). The headline is a figure of speech for signing without haggling, not a disclosure of terms — [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/sporarena/arda-guler-real-madrid-ile-sozlesme-uzatti-ispanyada-mansetler-patladi-bos-kagida-imza-atti-43383215)

### Inferences
- The AS "signed on a blank sheet" framing, together with the absence of an official figure, suggests the player did not push hard on terms. That is an interpretation of a headline, not a reported fact.

### Gaps
- No bonus structure was reported anywhere I found.
- I found no Marca or AS article giving a specific salary or clause figure for the November deal; the AS piece could not be fetched.
- I could not establish which original Spanish outlet produced the €8M figure.

## 4. Why did the club move now? Interest from other clubs, negotiation timeline, internal view

### Takeaway
The reported drivers were outside interest and his status as a core player:
- **PSG:** a reported €115M bid rejected in August 2026, and a rumoured €200M plus €22M-salary approach in November.
- **Saudi Arabia:** a reported €80M approach rejected.
- **Internal view:** Mourinho reportedly called him "intocable".
- **Wider plan:** a broader renewal push for young core players, Tchouaméni included.

The PSG figures are unconfirmed and come mainly from El Gol Digital.

### Cited Findings
- **Timeline per El Gol Digital:**
  - August 2026: Real Madrid rejected a €115M offer from PSG ("according to various reports").
  - Mid-November: PSG was reportedly ready to offer €200M (ten times the roughly €20M Madrid paid in 2023) plus a €22M salary.
  - Turkish press reported that Güler had rejected a proposal to 2035 seven days before the announcement.
  - Source: [El Gol Digital](https://www.elgoldigital.com/futbol/real-madrid-cf/arda-guler-renueva-real-madrid-2032)

## 5. Context: 2025-26 under Xabi Alonso and Arbeloa, coaching changes, and how Arda's role evolved

### Takeaway
Arda went from rotation player to regular starter under Xabi Alonso in 2025-26: by 23 Dec 2025 he was the only Madrid player to have featured in every match. After Alonso left in January 2026 (following the Supercopa final defeat to Barcelona), he stayed important under interim coach Arbeloa. Madrid ended 2025-26 without trophies, but Arda won the UCL 2025-26 young player award.

Under Mourinho (2026-27) he is a starter, used both as a right-sided attacker and as a future deep midfielder. He scored and assisted in a 3-0 win at Mestalla before the international break.

### Cited Findings
- **Under Xabi Alonso:** "Llega el momento de Arda Güler: Xabi Alonso le entrega el mando" — [Soccerway](https://es.soccerway.com/noticias/futbol-laliga-ea-sports-llega-el-momento-de-arda-guler-xabi-alonso-le-entrega-el-mando/WvNf0cGG). Official club item dated 23 Dec 2025: Arda was the only Madrid player to appear in every match that season — [realmadrid.com](https://www.realmadrid.com/es-ES/noticias/futbol/primer-equipo/actualidad/arda-guler-el-unico-madridista-que-ha-participado-en-todos-los-partidos-esta-temporada-23-12-2025)
- **Alonso's exit:** after the 3-2 Supercopa defeat to Barcelona, the club ended Alonso's tenure and appointed Arbeloa. Alonso's tenure lasted 233 days — [ESPN Deportes](https://espndeportes.espn.com/futbol/espana/nota/_/id/16178046/real-madrid-xabi-alonso-motivos-de-su-salida); [ESPN Deportes, cinco razones](https://espndeportes.espn.com/futbol/espana/nota/_/id/16175273/real-madrid-xabi-alonso-despido-cinco-razones). - **Madrid's 2025-26:** ended without trophies — [El Español, 11 Jun 2026](https://www.elespanol.com/deportes/futbol/20260611/mourinho-nuevo-entrenador-real-madrid-proximas-temporadas/1003744277242_0.html) / [NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/soccer/news/jose-mourinho-named-new-manager-of-real-madrid-what-will-he-do-to-turn-things-around)
- **Record at the renewal:**
  - 126 matches over four seasons and five trophies: La Liga, Supercopa de España, Champions League, UEFA Super Cup and Club World Cup (Intercontinental).
  - UCL 2025-26 young player / "Breakthrough" award in September 2026.
  - 38 caps for Turkey.
  - Sources: [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/deportes/futbol/20261118/real-madrid-blinda-arda-guler-anuncia-renovacion/1003744462627_0.html); [El Gol Digital](https://www.elgoldigital.com/futbol/real-madrid-cf/arda-guler-renueva-real-madrid-2032)

  - Expected to start in a midfield trio with Bernardo Silva and Valverde; has also played regularly on the right — [Trivela](https://trivela.com.br/espanha/arda-guler-mourinho-duvida-real-madrid-diomande-rodrygo/)

- He has gone from "promising reserve" (2023-25) to "every-match player" (Alonso), to starter (Arbeloa), to cornerstone (Mourinho). That arc, plus outside interest, explains why the club locked him in to 2032.

### Gaps
- Some sources conflict on whether he plays deeper or on the right this season.
- I did not verify his exact 2026-27 statistics to date.
- The exact date of Alonso's dismissal (January 2026) was not pinned down.
