# Real Madrid's "young core" strategy and scenarios after Arda Güler's 2032 extension

Research date: 2026-11-20. Labels used below: **[FACT]** is an official announcement or something several sources agree on. **[REPORT]** is a press claim that has not been confirmed, with the outlet named. **[OPINION]** is analysis or punditry. Where sources conflict, the conflict is stated.

Context from the sources (2026): José Mourinho returned as head coach on 11 June 2026, on a deal to 30 June 2029. He replaced Álvaro Arbeloa, who had taken over after Xabi Alonso was sacked. Madrid won no trophy in 2025-26.

## 1. The model: sign early, extend long, and current contract expiries (late 2026)

### Takeaway
Under Pérez, with chief scout Juni Calafat, Madrid sign elite teenagers at 16-18, often agreeing the deal before the player turns 18 (FIFA rules), on roughly six-year contracts. They then re-extend the ones who make it, so their expiries line up. In November 2026 that alignment was visible: Vinícius (Aug), Güler (18 Nov) and Tchouaméni (19 Nov) were all moved to 30 June 2032 within about three and a half months. GOAL reported that Valverde will follow, to 2031.

### Inferences
- 2032 is becoming the club's anchor year for this generation: Vinícius, Güler and Tchouaméni already, Bellingham reportedly targeted, and Valverde reportedly to 2031. This means no core player can run his contract down until at least 2029-31.
- Short extensions are given to veterans (Rüdiger and Courtois, one year each) and long ones to players aged 25 and under. That is a clear age-based contract policy.

## 2. Precedents: how renewals were handled, and who was sold (parallels to Arda)

### Takeaway
Players who became starters (Vinícius, Rodrygo, Valverde, Camavinga, Tchouaméni) were re-extended mid-contract, usually with two or more years still left. Young players who could not break into the first team were sold, often with buy-back clauses (Hakimi, Reguilón, Ødegaard, Nico Paz). Güler's path matches the "keep" branch: an extension with three years left, after he became a regular starter.

- [FACT] Vinícius's earlier 2027 deal was allowed to reach its final year before the Aug 2026 renewal. — [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/49546343/vinicius-junior-agrees-real-madrid-contract-arsenal)

### Inferences
- Güler resembles Ødegaard in profile: a teenage creative player bought from outside Spain who spent long spells out of the team. The difference is that Güler won a regular starting role (under Mourinho) before any loan or sale became necessary. Madrid extended him with three years left rather than letting him reach the final 18 months, which avoids the leverage problems seen with Vinícius in 2026.
- The buy-back pattern (Reguilón, Paz) shows the club's main alternative to long contracts: sell with a buy-back option to keep the upside. Güler's extension means he will not take that route.

The main branches in the coverage are these. (a) Güler becomes a fixed starter in a Mourinho midfield with Bellingham and Tchouaméni, possibly joined by Nico Paz (€80m buy-back, summer 2027 only). (b) The club sells one of its sellable young pieces: Rodrygo (recovering from an ACL tear, contract to 2028) and Camavinga (2029) appear most often. (c) Vinícius's question is settled for now by his 2032 renewal, but the attacking hierarchy (Mbappé, Vinícius, Endrick, Diomande, Güler on the right) remains disputed.

### Inferences
- **Branch A ("core of the core")**: Güler becomes the main midfield creator through his peak years. Pressure for a further renewal around 2029-30 would come at age 24-25, following the Valverde pattern.
- **Branch B ("Paz duplication")**: if Madrid trigger the €80m Paz option in summer 2027, there would be competition for the same position. The long contract protects Güler's resale value in that case, rather than guaranteeing him minutes.
- **Branch C ("funding sales")**: Camavinga and Rodrygo are the most often named sale candidates. Neither is tied to 2032, which fits the club's two-tier pattern of extending some players and selling others.
- **Coaching risk**: Mourinho's contract runs to 2029. Güler's role depends on a coach who has backed him, and a coaching change before 2032 is likely given Madrid's coaching turnover (Alonso, Arbeloa and Mourinho within about a year).
- Peak age: the contract covers ages 21-27, which leaves his likely peak years (commonly cited as about 26-29) only partly covered. A further renewal around 2029-30 is the logical next decision point.

## 5. Financial angle

### Takeaway
Madrid reported record 2025-26 revenue of €1.221bn, with the Bernabéu bringing in €363m.
