# Arda Güler: on-field trajectory 2023–2026 (performance and Turkey)

All figures are as of the date stated next to them. The research was done on 2026-11-20. Live stats sites update on different dates, so club figures from different sites conflict slightly. Each conflict is noted where it appears.

## 1. Season stats 2023-24 to 2026-27 and records

### Takeaway
At Real Madrid he went from a bit-part player (12 apps in 2023-24) to a 49–51-game regular in 2024-25 and 2025-26. In 2025-26 he had 14 assists, the team's highest total, and won UCL Young Player of the Season. 2026-27 is his most productive start so far: 4 goals and 6–7 assists in 14 games by mid-November 2026. He has 126 Real Madrid appearances in total.

### Inferences
- His output roughly doubled from 2024-25 (6 goals, assist total unverified) to 2025-26 (6 goals, 14 assists). The jump came when he moved from occasional winger to a regular central creator. This is the strongest statistical case for the extension.
- His 2026-27 pace is about one goal involvement every 76 minutes in LaLiga: 7 G+A in 744 minutes. That is career-best efficiency.

### Gaps

## 2. Role and position evolution

### Takeaway
Under Ancelotti he was mostly a substitute No.10 or right winger. Under Xabi Alonso (June 2025 – 12 Jan 2026) he became a regular starter in central midfield in an Özil-like role. Arbeloa (Jan–May 2026) kept him in the team, though they clashed publicly once. Since summer 2026, Mourinho has used him as a right-sided inverted playmaker or central No.10. Mourinho says Güler will move deeper over time, along a "Modrić/Bernardo Silva path."

### Cited Findings
- His position is listed as attacking midfielder who can also play winger, mainly on the right; he is left-footed. After Alonso arrived, he started more regularly, mainly in midfield — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arda_G%C3%BCler).
- Alonso made him a regular starter from the 2025 Club World Cup on. He played "a midfield role more akin to … Mesut Özil" rather than as a Messi-style creator — [Goal](https://www.goal.com/en/lists/arda-guler-kylian-mbappe-provider-in-chief-underrated-star-real-madrid-attack/bltbf4f088476617d09).
- Alonso left on 12 Jan 2026 after losing the Supercopa final 3-2 to Barcelona. Álvaro Arbeloa, the Castilla coach, took over — [beIN Sports](https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/copa-del-rey/articles/arbeloa-replaces-alonso-at-real-madrid-2026-01-12); [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13493479/xabi-alonso-real-madrid-head-coach-leaves-the-club).
- NTV Spor reported an on-camera outburst at Arbeloa during a Benfica match, with the remark "Always me" — [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/sporskor/galeri-arda-guler-cilgina-dondu-hocasina-soyledikleri-kameralara-yansidi-takimdan-ayrilma-vakti-1709517). I took the detail from a search summary only.
- **Mourinho (2026-27):**
  - He told El Chiringuito he expects Güler to develop into a deeper midfielder, comparing him to Modrić and Bernardo Silva — [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/sports/football/mourinho-backs-arda-guler-to-follow-modric-bernardo-silva-path).
  - Mundo Deportivo, via Football España, reported a plan to build the side around Güler in an Özil-type role — [Football España](https://www.football-espana.net/2026/07/23/arda-guler-real-madrid-mourinho-ozil-role).
  - Güler said: "It's a great opportunity to work with a legend like Mourinho" — [Real Madrid](https://www.realmadrid.com/en-US/news/football/first-team/latest-news/entrevista-arda-guler-25-07-2026).
  - Current use is right-sided inverted playmaker or CAM — [Turkish-Football.com](https://turkish-football.com/arda-guler-real-madrid-2026-27-stats-contract-turkey-guide/) (a fan site).
- Fanatik also reported early-season friction, a "Mourinho shock" story — [Fanatik](https://www.fanatik.com.tr/real-madrid/galeri-arda-gulere-jose-mourinho-soku-bas-agrisi-real-madridde-kriz-basladi-2631595). This came before his strong autumn form.

### Inferences
- Four head coaches in three-and-a-half seasons have all kept or increased his role. The trend runs from winger/No.10 toward a central or deep playmaker, which is the profile clubs tie to long contracts.

### Gaps
- I have no Ancelotti-era quotes or position heat-maps. I found no detail on how Arbeloa used him tactically.

## 3. 2026 World Cup (and the "match against Japan")

### Takeaway
**Turkey did not play Japan at the 2026 World Cup.** In Group D they lost 0-2 to Australia and 0-1 to Paraguay, then beat the USA 3-2. That made 3 points, 4th place and a group-stage exit. Güler scored 1 goal, the equaliser against the USA. Real Madrid reported he was named MVP of that match. The premise about Japan appears to be wrong and should not go in the report.

### Cited Findings
- **MD1, 13 Jun 2026 (14 Jun in some time zones), BC Place, Vancouver: Australia 2-0 Turkey.**
  - Goals: Irankunda 27', Metcalfe 75'.
  - Turkey had 72% possession and 30 shots to Australia's 9; Australia's keeper made 8 saves.
  - Sources: [AP via KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/51510966/australia-spoils-turkeys-return-to-the-world-cup-with-a-2-0-victory); [ESPN](https://www.espn.co.uk/football/story/_/id/49052326/crossDomain).

  - Montella: "I'm sad but I'm also very proud of my players."
  - Source: [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13553626/world-cup-2026-turkey-0-1-paraguay-vincenzo-montellas-side-eliminated-from-world-cup-after-back-to-back-defeats). beIN headlined that he was "ashamed" after the exit — [beIN](https://www.beinsports.com/en-nz/football/fifa-world-cup-2026/articles/guler-ashamed-after-turkiye-s-world-cup-exit-2026-06-20).

## 4. Turkey national team: caps, goals, leadership, coaches

### Takeaway
As of 15 Nov 2026 he had 38 caps and 8 goals. He is Turkey's creative focal point, but Hakan Çalhanoğlu is still listed as captain. Montella was sacked on 9 Oct 2026 after Nations League defeats. Sergen Yalçın was appointed on 20 Oct 2026 and has built the attack around Güler.

### Cited Findings
- **38 caps, 8 goals** (as of 15 Nov 2026). By year (caps-goals): 2022 1-0; 2023 3-1; 2024 14-2; 2025 8-3; 2026 12-2. Debut 19 Nov 2022 vs Czech Republic. He is not listed as captain; Çalhanoğlu is — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arda_G%C3%BCler).
- Euro 2024: a goal vs Georgia (3-1, POTM) and an assist vs Austria in the round of 16 (2-1), helping Turkey reach the quarter-finals — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arda_G%C3%BCler).
- 2026-27 Nations League A: 4 starts and 1 goal, a free kick in the 20th minute vs Belgium in İzmir on 12 Nov 2026 (lost 1-2). Kevin De Bruyne reportedly called him "one of the best footballers in the world" — [Turkish-Football.com](https://turkish-football.com/arda-guler-real-madrid-2026-27-stats-contract-turkey-guide/) (fan site; I did not find the De Bruyne quote anywhere else).
- Montella said after the World Cup "I won't resign; respect our players" — [Medyascope, 25 Jun 2026](https://medyascope.tv/2026/06/25/montella-istifa-etmeyecegim-oyuncularimiza-saygi-gosterin/). The TFF board ended his tenure on 9 Oct 2026, after three-goal defeats away to Belgium and Italy — [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/sporskor/galeri-a-milli-takimda-montella-donemi-sona-erdi-1745518); [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/sports/football/turkiye-sack-montella-after-dismal-run-tff-rocked-by-resignations).
- **Sergen Yalçın** was named head coach on 20 Oct 2026, on a deal reported as running to 2030 (four years) — [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/sporskor/galeri-tff-sergen-yalcini-resmen-duyurdu-1747091); [Fanatik EN](https://www.fanatik.com.tr/en/sergen-yalcin-has-been-appointed-as-the-new-coach-of-the-turkish-national-team-2641683).

### Inferences
- With Montella gone, "Montella's comments" on Güler are historical (2023 – Oct 2026). Yalçın's set-up puts even more of the creative load on Güler.
